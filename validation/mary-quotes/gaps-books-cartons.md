# Structural gap analysis — Godzilla classic estimator vs. Mary's E&M quotes
### Scope: books, cartons and big offset work
Estimates analyzed: 348988, 348484, 348946, 348472, 348627, 348440, 349049, 348478, 348747, 348317, 348352, 348228, 347866, 348795, 349108

Engine under test: `C:\dev\cd-packaging\src\lib\classic-estimate.ts`
(`ClassicForm` / `ClassicPart` / `PART_FIELD_KEYS` / `computePart()` / `computeClassic()` / `BINDERY_OPERATIONS` / `JOB_TYPES` / `COATING_TYPES`)

Question asked of every estimate: *could Mary reproduce this in Godzilla's classic estimator today?*
Answer for **all 15**: no — every one of them trips at least one blocker or major gap.

## Notes on things that already work

Verified present and correct against these quotes, so no gap logged:
- Work & turn plate/washup/makeready collapsing (`workAndTurn` → `max(side1, side2)`) — #348988, #347866, #348228 covers.
- Coating occupying a press unit without a plate (5 units / 4 plates) — #348988, #348747 (7 units / 6 plates on #348478).
- Sheets out of parent (`sheetsOutOfParent`) with `pricePerM` per parent — #348472, #348484, #348317, #348478.
- Per-quantity-tier outside pricing (`OutsidePurchase.amountsByTier`) — #348747's five tiers, #348946's two, #348478's two.
- Auto cartons at 35 lb max (`cartonsAuto`) and $0.93/carton material — matches E&M's carton counts and material dollars on every carton job.
- Paper handling 0.1 hr line — #348988, #348317, #348478, #348747, #348352, #347866, #348795, #348228 (all 0.1 hr).
- Editable non-standard markups (#348228 = 32/17/30/38; #348795 = 28/17/30/38) and 0% commission (#347866).
- Saddle stitch as its own machine line at $95 with Help — #348795, #348440, #348472, #349108, #347866.
- Fold at $48/hr — #348228 exact ($305.20 / 6.4 hr and $7,505.02 / 156.4 hr).
